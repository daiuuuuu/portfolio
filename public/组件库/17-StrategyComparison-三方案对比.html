03 // STRATEGY / 三方案并行设计
3-Agent Pipeline Topology
NODE_A: SWISS
NODE_B: EDITORIAL
NODE_C: DARK_TECH
OPT_01 PRIMARY

Swiss Mono

Grid-dominant brutalism. Maximum clarity, zero decoration. Relies entirely on typographic scale and negative space for hierarchy. Chosen as the final production direction.

OPT_02 REJECTED

Editorial Serif

High-contrast serif typography mixed with asymmetrical image grids. Deemed too "boutique agency" and insufficiently technical for a data infrastructure provider.

OPT_03 FLAGGED

Dark Tech

Standard dark mode with glowing accents and WebGL node visualizers. Failed quality audit due to generic AI-generated aesthetic artifacts.
